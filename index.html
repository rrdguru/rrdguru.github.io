01 / Research

Quality is more than a feature.

It is an experience people form across encounters, expectations and outcomes.

My research asks how customers experience product quality, how those judgments shape brand relationships over time, and what they mean for the choices people make.

04 / Teaching and learning

The point is not to remember a tool. It is to know when to trust it, when to question it, and what to do next.

Across marketing, analytics and research methods, I connect concepts to real decisions, evidence and consequences. Students should leave able to explain their reasoning, not merely reproduce a model.

01

Start with a real question

Begin with a decision, customer problem or research puzzle before introducing a framework.

02

Make reasoning visible

Use cases, demonstrations, data and discussion to show how conclusions are reached.

03

Build critical independence

Test assumptions, recognise uncertainty and identify where an answer may fail.

04

Use technology with purpose

AI and analytics should extend inquiry and judgment, not replace either one.
